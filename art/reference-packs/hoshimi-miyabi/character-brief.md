# Hoshimi Miyabi Character Brief

## Identity

- Character: Hoshimi Miyabi / 星见雅
- Franchise: Zenless Zone Zero / 绝区零
- Intended output: an original, small OpenPets desktop-pet interpretation
- Intended use: internal reference for a non-commercial prototype

## Verified Visual Observations

### Silhouette and proportions

- Read as a disciplined, upright sword user rather than a broad or heavy fighter.
- Preserve the highly recognizable long-eared, fox-like head silhouette.
- Keep the weapon and scabbard readable without allowing them to overwhelm the body at desktop-pet scale.

### Hair, face, and expression

- Default expression should be restrained, focused, and composed.
- Short lighter reactions may show a softer or mildly puzzled side, but should not become broadly comedic.

### Palette

- Do not lock exact sampled colors until official source images are locally reviewed.
- Preserve strong dark/light contrast and reserve bright frost-like accents for motion and attack readability.

### Clothing and accessories

- Treat exact garment layers, insignia, fasteners, and accessory placement as pending visual confirmation.
- Preserve the formal martial and Section 6 impression; avoid replacing it with generic fantasy armor.

### Weapon or signature prop

- A sheathed sword and controlled draw-slash motion are essential recognition cues.
- Preserve the relationship between hand, hilt, scabbard, and stance across animation frames.

### Movement and personality cues

- Motion should feel economical, trained, and precise.
- Idle animation should use minimal breathing, ear movement, or a small hand adjustment near the sword.
- Running should remain balanced and purposeful; attack-inspired flourishes should favor one decisive arc over repeated visual noise.

## Must-Preserve Features

- Long fox-like ears and compact upright silhouette.
- Reserved facial expression.
- Sword-and-scabbard relationship.
- Controlled draw-slash movement language.
- Frost-like accent effects used sparingly.

## Safe Animation Simplifications

- Reduce garment layers to two or three large readable masses.
- Merge small fasteners and insignia into simple color blocks.
- Shorten the weapon only enough to fit the `192x208` cell while preserving its direction.
- Use a single frost arc or brief glow instead of complex combat effects.
